Log note box on a phone

The box at the bottom of a task. Three ways to fix it.

Now

The field and the Log action button sit side by side, so on a 375px screen the field gets about half the width. The Email tab falls off its edge and the placeholder clips.

A. Stacked

Nothing sits beside the field. Tabs across the top, the words full width, then the buttons on one line underneath. Log action keeps its name.

B. Everything inside the field

One box, nothing outside it. Log action becomes a ＋ on the same line as the send arrow, so the field keeps the whole width and the dock gets shorter.

C. One line until you tap it

Closed it is a single bar, so it stops covering the task behind it. Tapping opens the full box. Shown closed, then open.
