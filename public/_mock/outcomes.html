Why this is worth doing

The current right pane is a record of what the app did — priority changed, due date changed, assignee changed. Nobody reads that. This is a record of what you decided, and every entry ends with a dated commitment, which is the thing that feeds the Follow up date, My Work, and the Monday review you already have. It also closes the hole we found earlier: 93 of 179 open tasks have no due date, because nothing ever forced the question.
