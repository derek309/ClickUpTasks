What this needs from you

One field on a task (the size) and one setting (hours in your working day, and which days you work). Nothing else. Tasks with no size are counted at half a day so the plan is never silently wrong, and shown with a faint marker so it is obvious which numbers are guesses.

The thing I would not build first: per-person capacity across the team. It doubles the model and you are the constraint right now, not Michaella.
